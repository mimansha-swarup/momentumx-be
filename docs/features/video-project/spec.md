# Video projects

A video project is the workspace for one video. It keeps the idea, script, selected hook, publishing title and packaging together so the creator can resume work and understand what needs attention.

## Creator flow

1. Choose an idea or bring a working title to create a project.
2. Follow the suggested next step: Research → Script → Hooks → Title → Packaging.
3. Jump to another step when useful, subject to the inputs that tool needs.
4. Revisit and revise work. Review warnings identify outputs based on older upstream content.
5. Export assets when ready, or delete a project that is no longer needed.

Creating a project completes its Research choice. Script completes when generated content is saved, Hooks and Title complete on selection, and Packaging completes when its supporting assets are ready. The suggested next action points to unfinished or outdated work, so it may differ from the last visited step.

## Revision and lifetime

Changing an upstream idea or asset can mark downstream work as needing review. The creator can regenerate it or deliberately keep it as-is; existing content is not discarded simply because it is outdated.

Several projects can use one idea while maintaining independent scripts. Regenerating that shared idea can affect all dependent projects. Deleting a project removes it from the active workspace without deleting its saved idea or other projects.

Projects belong to a channel, and channel membership determines access. Editors and owners can change work; viewers can read and export. Authorship does not give access outside channel membership.

## Scope

The pipeline tracks preparation, not a scheduled/published video lifecycle. Completing all steps does not upload a video or establish that the creator has reviewed its quality.
