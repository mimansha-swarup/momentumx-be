# Product scope and open work

MomentumX locally supports channel setup, research and ideas, project scripts, hooks, publishing titles and packaging. Creators can revise, regenerate and export their work. Shared channels provide owner/editor/viewer access, and optional credit allowances limit generation.

These are repository capabilities, not confirmation of production rollout or output quality. Deployment and provider/browser acceptance belong in [operational readiness](../multi-account/operational-readiness.md).

## Current boundaries

- Thumbnail output is a text brief. Image generation and delivery are not implemented.
- Credits track generation allowances. Checkout, paid subscriptions and payment webhooks are not implemented.
- Public YouTube signals personalize work; private analytics and automatic publishing are not available.
- Context can be refreshed on demand. There is no scheduled refresh policy.
- Text can be saved or regenerated. Directional AI editing, such as “make this shorter”, is future work.

## Decisions before a paid launch

Validate the complete flow with representative target creators: idea relevance, script usefulness, voice, factual grounding, packaging quality, time saved and cost. A technically valid response does not establish that the draft is good enough to publish.

Reconcile the [intended pricing and trial](./overview.md) with current credit allowances and the cost of any future image service. Decide actual entitlements before promising unlimited text or image quotas.

Choose and test acquisition channels for growth-stage creators. Watermarking, if introduced with images, is an upgrade incentive rather than the distribution plan.

Thumbnail images require a quality/cost decision before becoming part of the product. Production traffic protection and coordinated release checks remain operational gates in the readiness guide.

## Later directions

Potential extensions are a content calendar and scheduled/published lifecycle, reusable brand and creative assets, analytics-informed suggestions, a trend entry feed, cross-platform repurposing, and a broader agency workflow. External export destinations such as Google Docs or YouTube Studio are also future integrations.
